I'd Still Choose You. ❤️

If I could write our story a thousand times,
I'd still make every chapter lead to you.
Through every laugh,
every silly fight,
every quiet night,
every dream
and every ordinary day,

I'd still choose your hand to hold.
Maybe the universe gave me many people to meet,
but somehow it gave me you to love.

And that's the part of my story
I never want to change.

Happy Birthday, Yakshita.

But to me,
you'll always be my Cutie Pie. 🥹❤️
