My Letter To You...

My Cutie Pie,

I don't think words will ever be enough for everything I feel when I look at you.

Somewhere between our silly conversations, random pictures, little arguments, laughter and quiet moments, you became one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

I love your smile.
I love your weirdness.
I love your childish moments.
I love your craziness.
I love every version of you.

Your happiness is my biggest treasure.

I don't promise a perfect life.
I promise that I'll keep choosing you, learning you, supporting you and loving you.

Happy Birthday to the girl who made my world a little more colorful just by being in it.
