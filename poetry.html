For The Girl My Heart Keeps Choosing 🌸

In a world full of places,
somehow your arms became my home.

In a world full of people,
somehow my heart chose you alone.

You are my favorite hello,
my safest place to stay,
and the little reason
I smile a little more every day.

Nuvvu naa life lo vachina roju nundi,
naa prapancham konchem andham ayyindi.

Nee navvu choosthe chaalu,
naa roju motham beautiful aipothundi.

Nuvvu pakkana unte chaalu,
vere em kavali anipinchadu.

Naa heart ki nachina place okkate —
adi nee pakkane.

It's not just the big moments I adore,
but the quiet ones that leave me wanting more.

The way you look at me across the room,
the way you make all my worries unbloom.

You are the poetry I never knew how to write,
the brightest star in my darkest night.

Naa prathi aalochanalo nuvve unnav,
naa prathi anandhamlo nuvve unnav.

Yenni kastalu vachina, yentha dooram unna,
naa cheyi pattu, nenunnanu antu navvu.

Nee prematho naa jeevithaniki ardham vachindi,
Nuvve naa prapancham, naa Cutie Pie.
